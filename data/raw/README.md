# Source workbooks

These are the two user-supplied workbooks used for this project, uploaded unchanged apart from their download filenames. SHA-256 checksums and original filenames are recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

- [All-author institutional participation workbook](NeurIPS_2026_All_Author_Institutions.xlsx)
- [First-listed-author institution workbook](NeurIPS_2026_First_Author_Institutions.xlsx)

The all-author workbook cites the [NeurIPS 2026 export page](https://neurips.cc/Downloads/2026) and reports a 26 September 2026 snapshot. We have not independently revalidated the complete conference export. The first-listed-author workbook was processed on 29 September 2026; that processing date is not a new conference snapshot.

These workbooks are inputs to an independent visualization, not official conference rankings. The first-listed author comes from the supplied metadata and is not PDF-verified equal-contribution authorship. Track coverage includes different and unconfirmed tracks. See the [project methodology](../../README.md#source-and-limitations) and [first-author coverage notes](../../first-author/data/source_notes.json) before comparing figures.

Detailed cleaned institution tables are available for [all-author participation](../institutions.csv) and [first-listed authors](../../first-author/data/institutions.csv). View-specific CSV downloads include counts, percentages and the corresponding denominator. A percentage is a share of the institution–paper counts displayed in that chart, not a share of unique conference papers.
